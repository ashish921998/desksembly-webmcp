## Project Idea

- Initial idea: A WebMCP-enabled 3D planning and commerce workspace for designing pop-up shops, exhibition booths, or related product-filled spaces.
- Core interaction: A human supplies intent, taste, and judgment while an agent uses structured WebMCP tools to inspect a Three.js scene, search a catalog, place and revise objects, validate constraints, calculate budget, and prepare an actionable plan.
- Demo moment: A natural-language request visibly becomes a complete 3D layout through animated, stateful WebMCP tool calls, followed by human-directed revisions.
- Audience: Initially broad; professional pop-up shops, exhibition booths, and general room planning remain candidates to narrow during scope.
- Sponsor fit: The experience should be appealing to Shopify and useful or entertaining for Shopify sellers.
- Visual direction: A miniature 3D world with animated packages that arrive, unpack, and move smoothly into a scene or cart.
- Research requirement: Before finalizing the plan, investigate current WebMCP technical possibilities, public discussion, Shopify-oriented opportunities, and interaction/game inspirations.
- Defining requirement: The demo needs one unmistakable moment that could not be reproduced as an ordinary website workflow.
- Researched direction: A Shopify-backed living planogram where a shopper and their browser agent co-create a miniature product world; live products become a reviewed kit and then a real Shopify cart.
- Proposed impossible moment: Agent-held context such as a moodboard becomes a structured, animated Shopify scene and reconciled cart through dynamically composed WebMCP tools, without a bespoke storefront upload/chat integration.
- Participant approval: The agent-operated miniature commerce world is the confirmed direction.
- MVP world: A compact desk setup assembled from 3–5 products selected from one small catalog.
